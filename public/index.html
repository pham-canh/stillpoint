Camera and data

Stillpoint asks for camera access only after you press Begin. Frames are sent to the Stillpoint process on this computer over loopback, measured in memory, and dropped immediately. Nothing is recorded, saved, or uploaded. No account, analytics, or third-party AI is involved.

Pulse, breathing and HRV are estimated by the Presage SmartSpectra SDK. Expression is optional and can be turned off on the intro screen. The SDK contacts Presage only to authenticate the API key and check the subscription; the metrics themselves are computed on this device. SDK telemetry is explicitly disabled.

Closing the session stops the camera and discards the measurements. This is a wellness experience, not a medical device, and it does not diagnose or treat anything.
